zhuawen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

zhuawen is a Safari extension that extracts the text of a web page, converts between Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and lets you save or share the result as a text file.

We don't collect your data

zhuawen does not collect, store, or send any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

How the extension works

  • Text is extracted and converted entirely on your device.
  • When a page links to more parts of the same article or book, zhuawen can load those pages from the same website to combine them. These requests go only to the website you are reading, the same way Safari would load them.
  • Files you save or share go only where you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Website access

iOS asks you to allow zhuawen on websites. This permission is needed to read the page you are viewing. You can change it at any time in Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → zhuawen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: zhuawen.app@gmail.com

zhuawen 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

zhuawen 是一款 Safari 扩展，可提取网页正文，在简体与繁体中文之间转换，并将结果保存或分享为文本文件。

我们不收集你的数据

zhuawen 不会收集、存储或发送任何个人信息。没有账户，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有追踪。

扩展如何运作

  • 正文的提取与转换完全在你的设备上进行。
  • 当网页链接到同一文章或书籍的其他部分时，zhuawen 可以从同一网站载入这些页面并合并。这些请求只发送到你正在阅读的网站，与 Safari 浏览器正常载入页面的方式相同。
  • 你保存或分享的文件只会发送到你在 iOS 分享菜单中选择的位置。

网站访问权限

iOS 会请你允许 zhuawen 访问网站，这是读取当前网页所必需的。你可以随时在“设置” → “App” → “Safari浏览器” → “扩展” → “zhuawen”中更改。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，更新后的版本将发布在此页面并注明新的日期。
